# IC DAO Roadmap

## 🌍 Vision
IC DAO aims to redefine how people invest in real estate by combining blockchain transparency, community governance, and global accessibility.  
Our mission: **make property investing open, liquid, and borderless.**

---

## 🚀 Phase 1 — Foundation (Q4 2024 – Q1 2025)
**Goal:** Build the technical and organizational base for IC DAO.

- Token creation and Solana mainnet deployment ✅  
- DAO setup on Realms (verification in progress)  
- GitHub repository and governance documentation  
- Initial community building and early contributor recruitment  
- IC DAO landing page on [investincryptonews.com](https://investincryptonews.com)

🧩 *Outcome:* Verified DAO structure, live documentation, and token ready for liquidity launch.

---

## 💧 Phase 2 — Liquidity & Governance Launch (Q2 2025)
**Goal:** Activate DAO governance and enable trading/liquidity for $IC.

- Raydium liquidity pool creation (IC/SOL or IC/USDC pair)  
- First governance proposal and on-chain vote  
- Community airdrop and staking model  
- DAO treasury dashboard setup  

🧩 *Outcome:* Token trading live, governance proposals open, first DAO vote executed.

---

## 🏗 Phase 3 — Tokenized Property Pilot (Q3–Q4 2025)
**Goal:** Launch first tokenized real estate pilot program.

- Smart contract for property-backed token series  
- Partnership with legal and property management entities  
- Integration with NFT-based ownership certificates  
- Public transparency dashboard for investors  

🧩 *Outcome:* Real-world asset tokenization live on Solana.

---

## 🌐 Phase 4 — Expansion & Partnerships (2026)
**Goal:** Scale IC DAO globally and establish cross-chain presence.

- Institutional onboarding & DAO compliance (EU MiCA alignment)  
- Cross-chain deployment (Ethereum / Polygon bridges)  
- IC DAO Grants Program for real estate startups  
- Community governance scaling (voting delegation system)

🧩 *Outcome:* Global DAO participation and multi-chain integration.

---

## 🔮 Phase 5 — Global Impact (2027 and beyond)
**Goal:** Become the leading decentralized platform for real estate investment and governance.

- Real-world asset marketplace  
- DAO-based crowdfunding framework  
- Educational platform for tokenized investing  
- IC DAO Foundation establishment  

🧩 *Outcome:* Sustainable ecosystem bridging real-world value and decentralized finance.
